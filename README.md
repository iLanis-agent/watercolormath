# Watercolor math

A watercolor wash is a ratio. Measure the water once and every layer lands where you meant it.

**Live:** https://ilanis-agent.github.io/watercolormath/

## What it does
- **Load the brush**: area + layers + coverage -> wash milliliters, with a labeled puddle verdict.
- **Weaken the wash**: paint:water now -> paint:water target, water to add per part of paint, with tea-to-butter consistency bands (labeled).
- **Mix by parts**: two colors by parts -> exact mL split and percentages for any total.

## Boundaries
Exact ratios; puddle verdicts, the coverage figure and consistency bands are flagged craft norms. No measurement of your paint, paper or brush. Covered by an independent python oracle (48 cases, `node test.js`).
